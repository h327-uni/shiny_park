This app demonstrates the potential of VGI data to reveal more about the world around us. It explores a case study of bin placements within selected Auckland parks, while the dashboard statistics provide a base-level of analysis of the provided raw data. Users should note the potential risks associated with VGI data - locations may be imprecise, and descriptions may be misleading. Treat this dashboard as a proof-of-concept, rather than a factual presentation of the world around us. 

The dashboard can be useful for both local community members and those seeking to explore further the potential of VGI and the immense value of effective data visualisation. Local-oriented data, gathered by those who directly care about the environments being represented, will always offer unique benefits over outsourcing, which is one of VGI's core strengths. This dashboard demonstrates that capacity, while also providing those involved in its construction with valuable experience in data visualisation.
